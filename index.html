FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
We keep MOQ flexible: trial orders from 20–100 pieces per model depending on the product line, and mixed orders across models are always welcome — small starts are how most of our partners begin.
Can we mix different products in one order or container?
Yes — mixed containers are our specialty. Cameras, cables, drones and accessories can be combined in one shipment; we handle consolidation, labeling and a single set of export documents.
What is the lead time?
In-stock models ship in 3–7 working days. OEM/ODM orders with custom branding or packaging typically need 15–25 days depending on quantity.
Do you support OEM branding?
Yes. We customize logo printing, housing colors, retail boxes, manuals and firmware language. Low MOQ applies to first OEM orders.
What warranty do you provide?
All products carry a 12-month warranty. Dead-on-arrival units are replaced free of charge in your next order, and spare parts are stocked for every model we ship.
Are certificates available?
CE / FCC / RoHS documentation is available for most models — ask our team for the certificate package of the exact items you plan to import.
How do you ship, and can you deliver to Amazon FBA?
We ship by express, air and sea under FOB, CIF or DDP terms, and we are experienced with Amazon FBA deliveries including carton labeling and appointment handling.
What are your payment terms?
T/T with 30% deposit and balance against shipping documents is standard. L/C is available for established partners, and other terms can be discussed for long-term cooperation.
