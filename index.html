FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
Trial orders of 1 unit are welcome so you can test performance in your fields. Tiered distributor pricing starts from 5 units, and container-load programs include marketing support.
What is the lead time?
Standard configurations ship in 7–15 working days after payment. OEM/ODM orders with custom branding typically need 20–30 days depending on quantity.
What warranty and after-sales support do you provide?
Core components carry a 12-month warranty. We stock spare parts for every model we ship and provide lifetime remote technical support via WhatsApp, email and video call.
Do you provide operator training?
Yes — every order includes English manuals, operation and maintenance videos, plus live remote training sessions. On-site training at your location is available for fleet projects.
How are the drones packed and shipped?
Drones travel in custom flight cases with foam inserts. We ship by express, air or sea freight under FOB, CIF or DDP terms, with complete export documentation.
What are your payment terms?
T/T with 30% deposit and balance before shipment is standard. L/C is available for established partners, and other terms can be discussed for long-term cooperation.
Can we sell under our own brand?
Yes. OEM services cover logo, color scheme, packaging, ground-station language and even firmware customization, with low MOQ for first orders.
Which crops and scenarios are your drones suitable for?
Our spraying and spreading platforms serve rice paddies, wheat, cotton, maize, orchards, tea plantations, vegetables, forestry and ranchland — most field crops between 0.5 and 1,000+ hectares.
