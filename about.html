About MENGSUI

Based in Shunde, at the Heart of China's 3C Supply Chain

MENGSUI (Foshan Shunde Mengsui E-commerce Co., Ltd.) is a consumer electronics supplier based in Shunde District, Foshan City, Guangdong — a manufacturing belt famous for home appliances and 3C products, right next door to the electronics clusters of Shenzhen and Dongguan.

We work with vetted partner factories covering digital cameras, aerial imaging devices, charging accessories and shooting gear, and export to distributors, e-commerce sellers, retail chains and promotional-gift programs in more than a dozen markets.

  • One-stop sourcing: from cameras to cables in a single order
  • Mixed-container consolidation & inspection
  • Retail-ready packing: boxes, inserts, barcodes, manuals
  • Dedicated export team fluent in English
