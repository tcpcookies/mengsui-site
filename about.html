About MENGSUI

Rooted in the Pearl River Delta UAV Manufacturing Belt

MENGSUI (Foshan Shunde Mengsui E-commerce Co., Ltd.) is an agricultural drone exporter based in Shunde District, Foshan City, Guangdong — the heart of China's manufacturing belt and home to one of the world's densest UAV supply chains.

We integrate partner production lines covering airframe assembly, RTK integration, smart battery packs and flight testing, delivering complete plant protection solutions to distributors, agri-service fleet operators, plant protection service providers, cooperatives and government programs.

  • Dedicated export team fluent in English
  • Flexible OEM/ODM — from one unit to full fleet programs
  • Consolidated sourcing for drones, batteries, parts and chargers
  • Experience with tender and fleet documentation
